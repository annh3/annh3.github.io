Some guiding questions I have for pretraining are

1. How do you pre-train a foundation model so that it is easily adaptable in post-training? (Related to continual learning)?
2. How do you accomodate the computational needs of pre-training? (architectural optimizations, distributed training, efficient methods, hardware optimizations)?
3. What is a pre-trained model as a product?
